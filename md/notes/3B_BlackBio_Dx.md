## Business (2-3 lines)
Formerly Kilpest India (agrochemicals). The owner's son built the diagnostics arm (TRUPCR brand); it outgrew agrochem and the businesses merged into 3B BlackBio Dx Ltd (amalgamation completed Aug 2023). Now a pure-play molecular diagnostics kit manufacturer.

## My notes & thesis (Anurag's words — PRIMARY)
Growth thesis is European:
1. TRU-PCR — UK subsidiary in Manchester. "Made in UK" products, growing well, room to expand further.
2. Coris BioConcept (Belgium, recently acquired) — targeting the US market, awaiting FDA approvals. FDA approval = the biggest growth trigger.
3. Coris holds a Belgian government contract of ~€6M: ~€4M delivered, ~€2M to be delivered over the next two years.
