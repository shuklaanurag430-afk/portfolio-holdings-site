## My notes & thesis (Anurag's words — PRIMARY)
- Turnaround story. Sold via distributors/stockists → switched to direct hospital sales → hospitals paid late, trade receivables blew up (on-book revenue rose, cash conversion didn't) → switched back to stockists (better turnaround days).
- Cleanup cost: revenue fell ~₹20-22 Cr as hospital ties were cut. Receivables are now reducing.
- What drives the stock: (1) trade receivables < ₹260-270 Cr by end FY27 + continuous reduction in receivable days = proof the cleanup works; (2) Indore plant ramp (30-36% → 50-60-70% utilization) = revenue growth + working-capital improvement = price recovery.
- International = better margins on same products; CDMO/CMO = leverage built expertise. Primary growth engines are international + CDMO, but only if value gets unlocked.
