## Business (2-3 lines)
Carved out of unlisted P. N. Gadgil & Sons (193-year legacy name) in 2021. Sells branded fashion jewellery by piece, not weight, through 126 points of sale across 65 cities. Asset-light, zero-debt, franchisee-funded model. Mix: sterling silver ~56%, 14K natural diamond "UTSAV" ~34%, 9K gold ~3%.
