## Business (2-3 lines)
Branded hotel owner-operator in India: 38 hotels, 6,500+ rooms across 14 cities, run under Marriott, IHG and Hyatt flags. Owns the real estate; the chains supply guests via loyalty programmes and reservation systems. Anurag: a cyclical business; growth this year is from deleveraging, inorganic asset rebranding and capacity addition.

## My notes & thesis (PRIMARY — Anurag's words, synthesized)

### 2026-08-28

- Debt is reducing, but not by a lot — heavy capex in store.

- Seasonality: heavily Q4-weighted — Q4 provides a huge part of annual revenue.
