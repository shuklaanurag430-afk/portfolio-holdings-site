- **Management rating: Adequate.** High technical competence (Microsoft Inner Circle, all six solution-partner badges) and conservative capital allocation (zero debt, walked away from two bad M&A deals), but it missed FY26 growth targets and has not yet deployed the cash.

## 3. Future of the Company (2–3 year trajectory)

**Management guidance (label as such):** ₹500 Cr revenue in 4–5 years, with 30–40% from acquisitions and organic growth of 20–25%; margins back to 19–20% in 3–5 years.

**Analyst view (base case):** Organic growth of 12–16% as the US/Europe deal cycle normalizes, with OPM recovering from 12.5% toward 16–18% as the bench and AI investment get absorbed. This alone supports roughly ₹30–34 Cr PAT in 2 years, or ~₹15 EPS, which at a normalized 15–18x multiple implies ₹225–270.

**Analyst view (bull case):** A single $8M revenue acquisition (Microsoft-stack, US/Canada) adds 40–50% to revenue in one stroke and re-rates the stock toward peer multiples. This is the only scenario that justifies a materially higher price.

**Top 3 thesis-breaking risks, with quarterly leading indicators:**

- **Persistent US/Europe deal-cycle elongation.** Leading indicator: Services revenue QoQ growth. If international services stay flat or negative for two more quarters, the Q1 FY27 recovery is a false dawn.
- **Value-destructive M&A or persistent cash drag.** Leading indicator: cash balance and ROE. If cash stays parked at ₹149 Cr (earning ~6% on the balance sheet) while ROE stays at 15%, the "cash-rich" story becomes a value trap, not a catalyst.
- **Gross-margin dilution from product-mix shift without IP operating leverage.** Leading indicator: quarterly GPM vs OPM. Product share rose to 46% in Q1 FY27, compressing margins; if IP does not scale to the 10% target, the mix shift keeps OPM depressed.
