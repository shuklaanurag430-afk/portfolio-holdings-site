# SAMHI Hotels: A Correction That Was Fundamentally Earned and Technically Confirmed

## 1. Executive Summary: The Dual-Lens Diagnosis

SAMHI has corrected 38.2% from its July 2025 all-time high of ₹254.50 because the hotel upcycle matured (ADR growth collapsed, occupancy hit a ceiling, margins compressed) at the same time the balance sheet stayed levered and the growth pipeline moved back-loaded; the chart has been distributing the whole way down.

| Dead-cat bounce | Mar - Jun 2026 | ₹127.00 → ₹188.23 | Lower high |

**Reconciling the two lenses:**
- The stock began falling in July 2025, eight months before the Q4 FY26 miss was reported in May 2026. The market priced the deceleration in advance.
- The Q4 FY26 miss (operating profit -8.15% YoY, OPM 32.36% vs 38.11%) then confirmed the thesis, and the June 2026 bounce to ₹188.23 was sold into.
- Technically, the structure is textbook distribution: lower highs at ₹254.50 → ₹222 → ₹203.09 → ₹188.23, with price now below every key moving average.

## 2. Fundamental Deconstruction: What Actually Changed

### Answering the four questions directly

- **Did fundamentals change?** Yes, but as a maturation of the cycle, not a single event: growth shifted from rate-led to occupancy-led, ADR growth collapsed to ~2.6%, and occupancy hit a ~79% ceiling [1][2].
- **Did guidance change?** Effectively yes, by delivery rather than announcement: the ~38% FY27 EBITDA margin guidance is not being met (32.18% reported in Q1 FY27), and the 2.5x net debt/EBITDA target keeps sliding out [3][4].
- **Did quarterly numbers disappoint?** Yes, Q4 FY26 (reported May 2026) was the confirmation, but the stock had already fallen ~50% by then, meaning the market anticipated it.
- **Did strategy change direction?** No negative pivot. The buy-turnaround-rebrand business-hotel strategy is intact; the only new element is RARE India (asset-light leisure), which management itself flags will "suck up" capital at a 10-12% return zip code [2].

### Growth decelerated from rate-led to occupancy-led

- Q4 FY26 revenue growth slowed to 8.17% YoY, the weakest since Q1 FY26 and down from 15.63% in Q3 FY26 (Reported financials).
- Q1 FY27 revenue grew 12.12% YoY, still below the mid-teens run rate of FY25 (Reported financials).
- ADR growth was "largely flat" in Q1 FY27, with only a 2-3 percentage point improvement, and management confirmed most growth is now occupancy-led [2].
- Occupancy at 79.3% (Q1 FY27) is near a structural ceiling, with 36% of days above 90% [1], leaving little room to push volumes without price power.

### Margin compression: the guidance gap

- Q4 FY26 operating profit fell 8.15% YoY and reported EBITDA fell 6.0% YoY, with the Middle East conflict and GST changes as the stated drags [5][6].
- Q1 FY27 reported EBITDA fell 4.1% YoY; management cites 36% OPM ex-GST and "scope for improvement in subsequent periods" [3][4].
- Management's own FY27 projection is RevPAR growth of just 7-9%, down from 8-10% in FY26 and 12-14% in FY25 [7].

### The optical PAT confusion

- FY26 PAT of ~₹567 Cr (₹399.4 Cr in Q4 alone, +770.8% YoY) was inflated by a ~₹300 Cr deferred tax asset recognition, an impairment reversal, and the Caspia sale gain [5][6].
- This is why the stock trades at 6.09x P/E while falling: the earnings power is EBITDA minus finance costs and depreciation, not the headline PAT.
- The honest multiple is EV/EBITDA at 11.84x (Latest market data).

### Leverage and the capex overhang

- Net debt/EBITDA is 3.2x (2.4x on operating assets), and the 2.5x target keeps sliding out [3].
- The ₹2,200 Cr capex pipeline (W Hyderabad, Navi Mumbai, Noida, Chennai) means debt stays elevated even as EBITDA grows.

### Strategic direction: no pivot, but back-loaded

- Management reaffirmed the upscale share target of ~60% by FY30, with upscale inventory rising from ~41% [8].
- But the growth is back-loaded: W Hyderabad (Q4 FY27) is the only near-term catalyst; Navi Mumbai lands FY31.
- The result is a stock with zero near-term earnings catalysts and a levered balance sheet, which is precisely the profile the market has been de-rating.

## 5. Conclusion

The correction is fundamentally earned (growth deceleration, margin compression, optical PAT, leverage) and technically confirmed (lower highs, breakdown below key averages). The 6.09x P/E is an artefact of one-off accounting; the honest EV/EBITDA of 11.84x still prices in a back-loaded growth story that has not yet delivered.

**The single development that would change the technical view:** a weekly close above the 200-day EMA/SMA cluster (₹168-171) on strong volume, which would signal the distribution is over. Fundamentally, that would need to be backed by W Hyderabad commissioning on time (Q4 FY27) and a return of OPM toward the ~38% guidance.
